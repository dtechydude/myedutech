Student & Parent

Essential information for Parents and Students

How To Make Payments On The Portal?
First, make a transfer to the school's designated account. Afterward, login to the portal, navigate to "Payments/Bills" and select "Make Payments" to submit your proof and details.
How Do I Check My Results?
Results are located under "Exams & Results". You can view, print, or save them as a PDF directly to your device.
How Does The E-Learning Work?
Access class-specific lessons under the "E-Learning" menu. You can download materials, ask questions, and interact with teachers on the platform.
How About CBT?
Check the CBT Menu For your available exams. The School notifies you before the examination and send guidelines.
How Do I Update My Profile?
You can update basic information on your student portal. Use the "My Profile" menu on the left side menu bar to edit appropriately.

Staff & Admin

Understanding access levels and responsibilities

Subject Teacher's Role
Responsible for recording scores for specific assigned subjects and classes, managing e-learning contents and setting CBT questions.
Form Teacher's Role
Form teachers manage a specific class. They handle general attendance, comments on report cards, and oversee the overall academic welfare of students in their arm.
Superuser's Role
The Superuser has full administrative access to the portal. This includes managing school settings, academic sessions, financial configurations, and user permissions.
Admin/Officer Role
Assists with daily portal management, fee verification, and student registration. They serve as the primary contact for troubleshooting and data entry.
Miscellaneous Roles
Assinged to manage other roles like Hostel supervision, School bus management and route cordinations
