Student & Parent FAQs

What you need to know about the KwikSchools Portal

How Do I View Attendance Records?
Teachers mark attendance daily on the portal. Parents and students can go to the "Attendance" menu to see a day-by-day record showing Present, Absent, or Late, along with a summary total for the current term. If you notice an attendance entry that looks incorrect, contact your child's class/form teacher to have it corrected.
How Do CBT (Computer-Based Tests) Work?
When a teacher schedules a CBT (objective and/or theory questions) for a subject, it appears under the "CBT" menu as soon as it opens. Click the exam to begin — questions may be randomized, and the test auto-submits once the timer runs out, so make sure you have a stable internet connection before starting. Objective questions are graded automatically; your score and pass/fail status are added to your subject result once released by your teacher.
How Do I View My Report Card / Result?
Report cards are found under the "Results / Report Card" menu, broken down by session and term (First, Second, or Third Term). This includes Mid-Term reports, End-of-Term report cards, and the End-of-Session summary, each combining Continuous Assessment (CA) and exam scores, subject grades, class position, and psychomotor/affective ratings. Report cards can be viewed, printed, or downloaded as a PDF, but only become visible once the school has approved and released them for that term.
How Does The E-Learning Center Work?
The "E-Learning" menu lists every subject/class your child is enrolled in. Open a subject to access notes, slides, and video lessons uploaded by the teacher, download and submit assignments before their due date, and track how much of the term's material has been covered. Graded assignments and teacher feedback appear in the same place once marking is complete.
How Do I Pay School Fees, and Get an Invoice/Receipt?
Go to the "Finance / Payments" menu to view the current term's fee invoice, broken down by fee category (tuition, feeding, books, etc.), and any outstanding balance from previous terms. Where online payment is enabled, you can pay directly on the portal; otherwise, pay via the bank details provided and upload proof of payment for confirmation. Once a payment is verified, an official receipt is generated automatically and can be downloaded or printed from the same menu.
How Does School Bus / Transport Work?
If your child uses the school bus, the "Transport" menu shows their assigned route, bus number, and pickup/drop-off times and locations, along with the driver's or transport officer's contact details. Any change of route, delay, or transport-related announcement is also posted here and, where set up, sent as an SMS/email notification.
What About Hostel / Boarding Information?
For boarding students, the "Hostel" menu shows the assigned hostel/house and room, along with the house master's or matron's contact information. Visiting days, check-in/check-out dates for each term, and any hostel notices are also published here — check this menu ahead of resumption and closing dates.
How Do I Get School Announcements & Updates?
School-wide announcements, newsletters, and the school calendar (resumption dates, exam periods, events, and holidays) are all posted under the "Announcements" and "Calendar" menus. Where the school has enabled it, important updates are also sent to your registered phone number and email address by SMS or newsletter.
How Do I Update My Profile, or Get Help?
Basic details (contact number, address, passport photograph) can be updated under "My Profile". For anything else — a payment issue, a wrong result entry, or a general question — use the "Complaints/Support" menu to raise a ticket describing the issue, including your child's admission number where relevant. The relevant school office will respond through the portal or your registered contact details.

Teacher/Staff/Admin FAQs

All You Need To Know

Subject Teacher's Role
Teachers only see the classes and subjects assigned to them for the current session/term (see "My Classes"). From there you can mark daily attendance, upload notes/videos/assignments and grade submissions under "E-Learning", create CBT question banks and schedule tests, and enter Continuous Assessment (CA) and exam scores under "Results" for each subject you teach.
Class/Form Teacher's Role
A Class/Form Teacher is assigned oversight of a specific class arm (e.g. JSS 2 Gold, Primary 4 Blue). In addition to a subject teacher's usual access, a class teacher reviews and compiles report cards for every pupil/student in their class, adds overall psychomotor and affective remarks, tracks class attendance trends, and is usually the first point of contact for parents of that class.
Non-Academic Staff Role
Non-academic staff log in with access restricted to their unit. Bursary/accounts staff manage fee structures, invoices, receipts, and outstanding payment tracking under "Finance"; transport staff manage bus routes and pickup schedules under "Transport"; hostel staff (house masters/matrons) manage room assignments and boarding records under "Hostel". Each role only sees the tools relevant to its unit.
Admin/Proprietor Role
Admins/Proprietors manage core school data — sessions and terms, classes/arms (Nursery through Secondary), subjects, staff and student records, admissions and enrollment, and fee structures. Admins also configure role and permission access for staff, approve report cards for release, and can pull school-wide dashboard analytics and reports at any time.
How Do I Manage CBT & the Question Bank?
Under "CBT", teachers can create objective and/or theory question sets for their subject, set the exam window and timer, and enable question randomization. Objective questions are auto-graded on submission. Previously uploaded questions are kept in the "Question Bank", where they can be filtered by class, session, and term, then downloaded and reused for future exams instead of being re-typed from scratch.
How Do I Generate & Approve Report Cards?
Enter CA and exam scores under "Results" for each subject; the system automatically computes grades, class positions/ranking, and cumulative results across the term. Class teachers add psychomotor and affective ratings, then submit report cards for admin approval. Once approved, Mid-Term, End-of-Term, and End-of-Session report cards (and the class broad sheet) become available for printing, PDF export, and parent viewing.
How Do I Handle Fees, Invoices & Payment Tracking?
Bursary/accounts staff set up fee categories and structures per class under "Finance", generate termly invoices for all students, and confirm payments made online or by bank transfer, which automatically issues a receipt to the parent. The same menu shows outstanding balances per student or class, and can generate financial summary reports for the school.
How Do I Handle Parent/Student Complaints?
Complaints submitted through the "Complaints/Support" menu route to the relevant unit based on the category selected (academic, finance, transport, hostel, etc.). Check "Complaints/Support" → "Assigned To Me" to view, respond to, and mark complaints resolved. Escalate anything outside your unit's scope to the admin office.
