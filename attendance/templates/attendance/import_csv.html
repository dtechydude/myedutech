What This Import Does
  • 1Looks up each student by USN in your Django database.
  • 2Creates or updates one Attendance record per student per date.
  • 3Recalculates AttendanceSummary.days_present for the selected term.
  • 4days_absent & attendance_percentage are auto-derived — no manual entry.
  • ✓Idempotent — safe to re-upload the same file.
