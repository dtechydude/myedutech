How to Use

Steps

1
Download the template below and open it in Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet app.
2
Fill in student data — one student per row. Do not change or delete column headers.
3
Save as CSV (File → Save as → CSV UTF-8) and upload using the form above.
4
Review the results after upload. Rows with errors are listed so you can fix and re-upload.

Valid Values

student_type: day_student, boarder
student_status: active, inactive, graduated, dropped, expelled, suspended
blood_group: A+, A-, B+, B-, AB+, AB-, O+, O-
genotype: AA, AS, SS, AC, SC
relationship: parent, father, mother, sister, brother, aunt, uncle, other
DOB / date_admitted: YYYY-MM-DD or DD/MM/YYYY
current_class: must match exactly a class name in the system (e.g. JSS 1)
